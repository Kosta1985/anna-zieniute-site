Privacy & contact

We respect your privacy and only ask for the information needed to discuss your enquiry.

How enquiries work

Our contact form creates an SMS draft on your device. It does not automatically send the message or store the form submission on this website. You choose whether to send it through your mobile messaging provider. You can also call us directly.

Information you share

When you contact us by phone or SMS, you may share your name, phone number, suburb, your dog's details, and a description of the support you want. We use that information to respond, discuss availability and prices, and arrange services. Please do not send payment card details or unnecessary sensitive personal information by SMS.

Third-party services

Website hosting providers may process technical access logs. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and dog photographs from Unsplash, which may receive technical browser information subject to their own policies.

Contact or ask about your information

For questions about your enquiry or to request a correction or deletion of details you've sent us, phone 0481 861 461.

This is an introductory website privacy notice and should be reviewed once a final legal entity, booking system and payment provider are selected.
