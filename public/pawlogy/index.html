CURIOUS PAWS, GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you might be wondering.

Still have a question? We'd love to hear it.

Give us a call ↗
Can you help with barking, pulling or reactive behaviour?+

These are common reasons people get in touch. Share what's happening and we'll discuss suitable training support. Some behaviour concerns need veterinary assessment first, especially if there are sudden changes, pain or serious safety concerns.

Do you visit clients at home?+

Yes, home visits are part of our planned service offering, subject to trainer availability in your area. Online consultations are also available.

Can I discuss the price before booking?+

Absolutely. We know every situation is different. Tell us about your dog, your location and your budget. We're happy to discuss the price and confirm the total before you commit.

Can you dog sit for a day or for a few weeks?+

You can enquire about daily, overnight and longer-term dog sitting. We'll confirm availability, location, the care arrangement and a tailored quote before accepting a booking.

Are there extra travel fees for Gold Coast or Sunshine Coast?+

Depending on your suburb and the available team member, a travel charge may apply. We'll always tell you about it in advance.

How do I make an enquiry?+

Call 0481 861 461 or use the form below to create a pre-filled SMS. On your phone, tap Send in your messaging app to complete the enquiry.
